MeshSat 0.2.0 supports a node with a RockBLOCK 9704 and the kits' encrypted satellite messages. Update the node's firmware over Bluetooth, and switch its LED and screen from Settings. People shows your organisation's directory from the Hub. Text other MeshSat apps and kits through the Hub, and answer a TAK dispatcher's chat. An SOS is paged once whichever routes carry it, and its cancel always arrives. Greek, accented and emoji text in the kits' SMS format reads back as sent.
