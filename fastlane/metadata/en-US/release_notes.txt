MeshSat 0.3.0 brings the Android app's latest. Home shows your node's satellite beacon, and the Node health card says what it is doing. A public warning a MeshSat kit relays over the mesh sounds an alarm and stays under Setup > Safety > Warnings. Add a field kit on your network under Setup. Send feedback from Setup > Help > Support, with an optional report of the app's logs. Nothing goes online before you ask: the map loads when you open it, orbit data once your node's modem is seen. MeshSat asks for notifications once, so SOS and warnings can show.
