MeshSat 0.5.0 can keep the gateway running in the background, as MeshSat Android does. Switch on Keep the gateway running in the background under Setup > Advanced > Diagnostics and allow location Always: the phone then keeps reporting its position to your Hub and TAK, keeps the check-in timer and an SOS supplied with a current position, and keeps relaying while MeshSat is not on screen. iOS shows its blue location indicator meanwhile, and the battery drains faster. If location is allowed only while the app is open, the setting says so and opens iOS Settings for you.
