MeshSat 0.4.1 is a Reticulum and LXMF node, as Sideband and MeshChat speak it: write to an LXMF address directly, opportunistically or through a propagation node, over TCP, a Sideband on the same WiFi, or the node's RockBLOCK 9704 by satellite. In a Reticulum chat, hold the microphone to send a voice note, or add a photo. An RNode LoRa radio works over Bluetooth. Incoming messages now show as notifications, the SOS notification has Cancel, and a welcome page says what each permission is for. The mailbox check asks before it spends a credit, and the status strip shows the Hub, location and the node's 9704. A short press on the voice note microphone no longer keeps recording.
