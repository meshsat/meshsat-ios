MeshSat is a gateway app for off-grid messaging: no account or login in the app, no in-app purchase, no paid content. 0.2.0 was approved on 28 Oct 2026; its flows are unchanged in 0.5.1.

NEW IN 0.5.1, BACKGROUND LOCATION (2.5.4, raised for 0.1.0): one opt-in feature brings the location background mode back. Setup > Advanced > Diagnostics > "Keep the gateway running in the background", off by default. With it on and location allowed Always, the phone keeps reporting its position to the user's own MeshSat Hub and TAK server, keeps the check-in timer and an SOS supplied with a current position, and keeps relaying messages while the app is off screen, as a field gateway does; iOS shows its blue indicator. The video shows it after the hardware part: the switch, the home screen with the indicator while the phone moves, then the map, where the phone's position moved along the route while the app was in the background.

IN 0.4.0 (reviewed): Reticulum and LXMF messaging (Messages > Reticulum; any Reticulum server on TCP 4242 to try it), voice notes (microphone and speech recognition asked on first use) and photos (system picker) in a Reticulum chat.

THE HARDWARE: the MeshSat node is open hardware, a LILYGO T-Beam Supreme with a RockBLOCK 9603 or 9704 Iridium modem (https://github.com/meshsat/meshsat-fieldkit). Nothing is sold in the app; it talks to the node over Bluetooth LE.

PURPOSE: messaging without mobile network or internet (LoRa mesh, Iridium, Reticulum), offline map, SOS on every route; for hikers, sailors, radio amateurs, volunteer rescue groups.

SETUP: no credentials needed in the app. Without a node: Home lists each route; Passes works offline; Setup > Safety > "Test the alarm" runs the SOS flow without contacting anyone. With a node: Setup > Your MeshSat node > Continue (Bluetooth prompt, first time) > Scan for Meshtastic devices > tap the node > its Bluetooth code. iOS does not let an app send SMS, so MeshSat opens the Messages composer and the user taps Send.

EXTERNAL SERVICES, all optional: a Reticulum server over TCP and a listener for peers on the same WiFi, only when set up; a MeshSat Hub, self-hosted (open source) or hub.meshsat.net run by us (accounts by approval); GitHub, only when the user updates a node's firmware; OpenStreetMap tiles (ODbL); Iridium SBD through the node's modem; APRS-IS or a KISS TNC; CelesTrak orbit data, bundled; fetched once a node's modem is seen. No payment, analytics, advertising or AI service; the text compressor is a small ONNX model in the app.

REGIONS: everywhere but mainland China (ICP filing). No regulated material; OpenStreetMap (ODbL), Meshtastic protocol definitions (GPL-3.0). App GPL-3.0: https://github.com/meshsat/meshsat-ios. Privacy: https://meshsat.net/privacy/.
