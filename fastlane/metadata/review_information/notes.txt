DEMO VIDEO WITH THE HARDWARE (2.1, 6 Oct): attached, 1:29, iPhone 11 Pro Max, iOS 27.0, this build; left: photos of the node beside the phone, right: the phone's screen. Node unpaired at the start: launch, Setup > Your MeshSat node > Scan for Meshtastic devices, tap node FLNR_074c, iOS's Bluetooth pairing code, connected. About 12 s after a new bond the node refreshes its service table and the link drops once; the app reconnects by itself. Then Satellite (the node's Iridium modem), Home, a text to the mesh, Map, People, the node's live log.

THE HARDWARE: the MeshSat node is open hardware, a LILYGO T-Beam Supreme (Meshtastic LoRa radio) with a RockBLOCK 9603 Iridium modem; build instructions https://github.com/meshsat/meshsat-fieldkit, firmware https://github.com/meshsat/meshsat-firmware. We sell nothing. The radios are in the node; the app talks to it over Bluetooth LE.

PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE: an iPhone as a gateway for messaging without mobile network or internet: text over a LoRa mesh and by Iridium satellite, satellite pass prediction, offline map, SOS on every route. For hikers, sailors, remote workers, radio amateurs, volunteer emergency and search-and-rescue groups.

SETUP: no credentials needed in the app. Without a node: Home lists each route; Passes works offline; Setup > Safety > "Test the alarm" runs the SOS flow without contacting anyone. With a node: Setup > Your MeshSat node > Continue (Bluetooth prompt, first time) > Scan for Meshtastic devices > tap the node > its Bluetooth code. iOS does not let an app send SMS, so MeshSat opens the Messages composer and the user taps Send.

EXTERNAL SERVICES, all optional: a MeshSat Hub, self-hosted (open source) or hub.meshsat.net run by us (accounts by approval; demo login above); OpenStreetMap tiles (ODbL); Iridium SBD through the node's modem; APRS-IS or a KISS TNC; CelesTrak orbit data, bundled offline. No payment, analytics, advertising or AI service; the text compressor is a small ONNX model in the app.

REGIONS: the same everywhere; mainland China excluded only for the ICP filing. REGULATED/PROTECTED MATERIAL: none; OpenStreetMap (ODbL) and Meshtastic protocol definitions (GPL-3.0). App GPL-3.0: https://github.com/meshsat/meshsat-ios. Privacy: https://meshsat.net/privacy/.

EARLIER POINTS: no location background mode; in the background the position comes from the significant-change service (2.5.4). Before Bluetooth is decided the node page explains and its button reads Continue; denied offers Settings (5.1.1). No Android references (2.3.10).
